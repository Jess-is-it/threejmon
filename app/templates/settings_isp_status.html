Capacity Detection

Example: with a 90 to 105 Mbps window for 10 minutes, every observed peak in the completed window must stay inside that range before the ISP is treated as a possible 100M circuit. Readings below 90 Mbps remain Observing because low utilization cannot determine circuit capacity. Traffic above 105 Mbps is treated as 1G because a 100M circuit should not exceed that ceiling.
Default 4h. The average rule can confirm 100M only after the full average period and while the current short window contains no reading outside the min/max range.
The schedule uses Asia/Manila time. Above-ceiling traffic can still prove 1G during this window.
Overnight ranges are supported, for example 22:00 to 06:00. Matching start and end times disable the interval.
